# JPML Competition Rules Comparison (2026)

This is an unofficial English translation and reorganization of the Japan
Professional Mahjong League (JPML) 2026 competition-rules comparison.

- Source: [JPML competition rules](https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/activity/game_rule.html)
- Published sheet: `20260401_Competition Rules 0.7`
- Accessed: 2026-08-30
- Compared rulesets: **JPML Official**, **WRC**, **WRC-R**, and **M-League**
- The source labels M-League as reference material, not as a JPML ruleset.

The source uses a left arrow to mean "same as the ruleset immediately to the
left." Those arrows are expanded below so every column is self-contained. A
dash means that the item is not applicable.

## Translation Notes

- _Houtei_ here refers to the final discard of the hand.
- A player who declines a legal win after declaring riichi becomes furiten.
- A post-riichi ankan must not change the hand's tile-group structure or reduce
  its yaku.
- WRC-R and M-League use one red `5m`, one red `5p`, and one red `5s`.
- Double and triple ron are resolved by atamahane rather than multiple wins.
- A fifth kan declaration is prohibited instead of ending the hand after four
  kans.
- Ryuuiisou does not require the green dragon.
- Multiple yakuman are capped at four yakuman.
- Under JPML Official rules, 4 han 30 fu is not rounded up to mangan.
- Under the JPML Official, WRC, and WRC-R columns, 13 or more han is capped at
  fourfold mangan and there is no fivefold-or-higher counted limit.

## Scope

This document translates the comparison published by JPML. It is not a full
procedural rulebook and should not be treated as an official English edition.
Items absent from the source table remain unspecified here.
